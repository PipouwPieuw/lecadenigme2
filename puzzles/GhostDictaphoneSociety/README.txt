Ghost Dictaphone Society
=========================

From CADENIGME2/GhostDictaphoneSociety:

    npx serve .

Open both pages with the same seed (default 1):

    http://localhost:3000/ravabulle.html?seed=1
    http://localhost:3000/troncadelle.html?seed=1

Ravabulle encodes vowel, contour, and duration. Troncadelle plays the cylinders
and sets each ghost plus its offerings. A wrong strip stays put. Troncadelle's
screen stays dark until every cylinder is right. "Réglé" only means a cylinder
has a stored choice.

The archive word is Ravabulle's half followed by Troncadelle's half.
Placeholder halves come from the seed. They are not a campaign password.
